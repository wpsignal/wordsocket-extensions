**0.7.0** - Cart history (Phase 60). The cart rows move from the `shopsocket_carts` transient to three tables of the plugin's own (`{prefix}shopsocket_carts`, `_cart_items`, `_journeys`, created by `dbDelta` under `shopsocket_db_version`): a row per cart with its lifecycle (`first_seen`, `last_seen`, `left_at`, `converted_at`, `order_id`), items in their own table so `count_in_carts()` is an indexed query and two shoppers adding at once no longer overwrite each other. Presence rides WordSocket 0.30's `WPS.presence.track()`: the storefront only adds its cart id `b`, and WordSocket keeps the journey (`kind:id:ms` steps, `away` while the tab is hidden) and reports each visit and leave, which `inc/history.php` takes from the `wpsignal_visitor_visit` and `wpsignal_visitor_leave` actions to stamp `left_at` and, with history on, write the journey. The WooCommerce pages are named `shop`, `cart`, `checkout` and `thanks` through `wpsignal_page_step`. Conversion is stamped on `woocommerce_checkout_order_created` and `woocommerce_store_api_checkout_order_processed`. The board's Storefront Customers tile and the Customers row say "(N away)", and a "Recently abandoned" panel lists the latest 50 carts whose shopper left (products, value, when, and the order if one followed), from `abandoned` in `GET /shopsocket/v1/dashboard` and in `woo.carts`. New settings on the ShopSocket tab, card "Cart history": "Keep a history of carts" (off by default; off means the tables hold what the transient held, for the session, with no user or visitor ids), "Keep carts for [30] days" and "Keep journeys for [12] months". Journeys are archived anonymized (visitor and cart ids, post and term ids, durations, money; never a user id), one row per visit, grouped by the browser's visitor id. Pruning rides `woocommerce_cleanup_sessions`. A WooCommerce privacy exporter and eraser cover a logged-in shopper's cart rows and the journeys reachable through them; `uninstall.php` drops the tables and the option. Requires WordSocket 0.30.

**0.5.1** - The board recovers orders it never heard about. The relay stores no events, so an order placed (or a status changed) while the board's connection was down, or whose publish never left WordPress, did not appear until the page was reloaded: the board's refresh covered carts and the online count only. `GET /shopsocket/v1/dashboard` now answers with `as_of` (the server clock) and, when the request carries `?since=<as_of>`, with `orders`: those created or changed since then, at most 50, in the shape of an order event (`board_orders()` in `inc/dashboard.php`, which also serves the first paint; `BOARD_ORDERS` moved there from `admin-board.php`). The board sends the `as_of` of its last successful refresh on every one (the 30 second poll, 1.5 seconds after a reconnect, and on returning to the tab) and merges what comes back (`reconcile` in `useLiveOrders.ts`): a missing order is added at the top and chimes once per refresh if it was created in that window, a changed one updates in place, and a row an event touched after the request left is not overwritten by the older response.
