**0.1.0** (in progress, root Phase 64) - First release. Two post types: `postsocket_liveblog` (public, URL base `live`, meta `_postsocket_state`, `_postsocket_started`, `_postsocket_ended`) and `postsocket_entry` (non-public, `post_parent` the live blog, REST field `liveblog`, meta `_postsocket_pinned`, link = the live blog's `#postsocket-{id}` anchor); publishing an entry without a live blog is refused; trash, untrash and delete cascade; list tables get a Live blog column and filter and an Entries column; editor panels "Live blog" (selector, pin) and "Live coverage" (state, count, Add entry). The feed (`render_feed()`, the `postsocket/live-feed` block, auto-appended after the intro on classic and block themes, never in excerpts, REST or the relay render), `LiveBlogPosting` JSON-LD, `GET /postsocket/v1/liveblog/{id}/entries` paged by a `(before, before_id)` cursor. Realtime through WordSocket 0.32 triggers: `postsocket.entry.updated` (`save_post_postsocket_entry` and the pin meta hooks), `postsocket.entry.removed` (`transition_post_status` off publish, `before_delete_post`), `postsocket.liveblog.state`, on `postsocket:liveblog:{id}`; `postsocket.post.updated` on `postsocket:post:{id}` for live blogs and the enabled types; a rendered-content hash guard (`_postsocket_content_hash`, forgotten when a post leaves publish), payloads carry `assets` (block styles and view scripts) and `html` inline under 32 KB (`postsocket_inline_limit`), else `fetch: true` and the cacheable `GET /postsocket/v1/content/{id}?v=`. Reader modules (`tsc` emit, imported by module id): `feed.ts` (insert by date and id, FLIP, the "N new updates" pill, load older, catch-up), `living.ts` (hash-compared swaps, catch-up on a stale page and on reconnect), `morph.ts` (keyed reconciliation ignoring the front end's loading hints, view transitions), `assets.ts`, `relay.ts`. Settings: one option `postsocket_settings` (`living_types` empty by default, `entries_per_page`, `show_author`, `show_times`, `rewrite_slug`), `GET/POST /postsocket/v1/settings`, the PostSocket tab and the Extensions card.
